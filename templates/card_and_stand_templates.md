# NFC Hardware Print Templates & Production Specifications

This document provides exact physical dimensions, color codes, vector assets, and print-ready specifications for producing branded Google Review NFC cards and acrylic countertop displays in Saskatoon.

---

## 1. Physical Sizing & Bleed Specifications

### Standard NFC Card (CR80 Credit Card Size)
- **Trim Size**: 85.6 mm × 53.98 mm (3.370 in × 2.125 in)
- **Bleed Size**: 88.6 mm × 57.0 mm (add 1.5 mm bleed on all 4 sides)
- **Safe Zone**: 80.0 mm × 48.0 mm (keep all text and QR codes inside this margin)
- **Corner Radius**: 3.18 mm (1/8 inch rounded corners)
- **Material**: 0.76 mm solid PVC or 0.15 mm high-gloss vinyl sticker overlay with permanent adhesive.

### Acrylic Countertop L-Stand (4" × 6" Vertical)
- **Trim Size**: 101.6 mm × 152.4 mm (4.0 in × 6.0 in)
- **Bleed Size**: 107.6 mm × 158.4 mm (3 mm bleed)
- **Safe Zone**: 92.0 mm × 142.0 mm
- **Material**: 3 mm crystal-clear cast acrylic with pre-drilled or adhesive NFC puck backing.
