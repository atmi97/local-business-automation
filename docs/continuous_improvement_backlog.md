# Continuous Improvement Backlog & Parked Innovation Ideas

**Purpose**: This document tracks ideas, feature prototypes, and hardware experiments that have been intentionally parked to keep the initial sales and onboarding process 100% lean, low-friction, and zero-waste.

---

## 1. Parked: Custom On-Demand Sticker Printing Studio

### Why It Was Parked:
- **Zero Value to Validation**: Printing custom branded vinyl stickers for a business *before* they have committed to buying adds unnecessary friction, takes extra hours, and costs money upfront with the risk of discarded inventory.
- **The Lean Alternative**: Test with **clean, high-gloss generic countertop stands** (e.g. "Review Us on Google ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ - Tap Phone Here") that can be reused across any prospect during 14-day free trials.
- **When to Re-Activate**: Only offer custom laser-printed or UV-embossed branded stands as a **Tier 2 or Tier 3 retention bonus** once a business has paid their 2nd month of software subscription.

## 3. Decision Framework for Activating Parked Ideas

Before taking any parked item out of this backlog, it must pass the **Hormozi Friction Filter**:
1. *Does this increase the perceived likelihood of achievement for the customer?*
2. *Does this shorten time to their first 5-star review?*
3. *Does this decrease effort & sacrifice for the agency or the business owner?*

If an idea adds operational complexity without directly generating new reviews or increasing subscription retention, it remains in this backlog.
