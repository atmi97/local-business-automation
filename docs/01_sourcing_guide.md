## 1. NFC Chip Technology: What to Buy & What to Avoid

When buying NFC hardware, you will encounter three primary chip models. Selecting the wrong one will limit URL length and cause tracking failures.

| Chip Model | Memory Size | Max URL Length | iOS/Android Compatibility | Agency Recommendation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **NTAG213** | 144 Bytes | ~130 characters | 100% (iPhone 7+ / Android) | ⚠️ **Passable for short URLs**, but fails if you add UTM parameters or tracking queries. |
| **NTAG215** | 504 Bytes | ~490 characters | 100% (Universal) | ⭐️ **BEST VALUE (Recommended)**. Plenty of space for dynamic URLs, device IDs, and campaign parameters. High availability (same chip used in Nintendo Amiibos). |
| **NTAG216** | 888 Bytes | ~850 characters | 100% (Universal) | ⭐️ **PREMIUM OPTION**. Great if you want to store vCards (contact details) or dual payloads. Slightly higher cost. |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Operating Frequency**: Must be **13.56 MHz** (Standard: ISO 14443-A).
> Do NOT buy 125 kHz RFID cards (those are for apartment/door access and cannot be read by smartphones).

## 2. Hardware Form Factors & Best Use Cases

1. **Acrylic Countertop L-Stands (10 cm × 15 cm or 8 cm × 12 cm)**
   - *Best for*: Restaurant host stands, checkout registers, dental/chiro reception desks, salon payment counters.
   - *Why*: Visible at eye level, stable, durable, and hard for a customer to accidentally walk away with.
2. **PVC / Matte Plastic Cards (Standard Credit Card Size: 85.5 mm × 54 mm)**
   - *Best for*: Mobile service businesses (HVAC technicians, mobile auto detailers, plumbers, realtors, personal trainers) or waitstaff carrying them in aprons.
   - *Why*: Fits in pockets, durable, waterproof.
3. **Adhesive NFC Table Pucks / Epoxy Coins (30 mm – 50 mm)**
   - *Best for*: Bars, Korean BBQ tables, breweries, food courts where patrons sit for extended periods.
   - *Why*: Peel-and-stick backing directly to the center of table tops or salt/pepper caddies.
